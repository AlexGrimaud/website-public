Inflation · Expectations · Monetary policy

Understanding inflation.
Informing monetary policy.

How do expectations and price-setting decisions shape inflation? And why does monetary policy work differently across the business cycle?

I am a research economist in the Monetary Policy Section at the Oesterreichische Nationalbank, the central bank of Austria. My research studies these questions using structural macroeconomic models, with a focus on nonlinear and state-dependent dynamics.

I received a joint PhD in Economics from the University of Amsterdam and the Catholic University of Milan in 2021. Previously, I held positions at WU Vienna, TU Wien and the Polytechnic University of Milan.

Short biography

Alex Grimaud is a research economist in the Monetary Policy Section at the Oesterreichische Nationalbank. His research focuses on inflation dynamics, monetary policy and expectations formation, using structural macroeconomic models with nonlinear and state-dependent dynamics. He received a joint PhD in Economics from the University of Amsterdam and the Catholic University of Milan in 2021.
